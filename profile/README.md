# HLTLab

**AI integration and custom systems for small businesses.**

We help small businesses take advantage of AI — setting up practical systems,
integrating tools that already exist, and building custom solutions where
off-the-shelf software falls short.

### What we do

- **AI integration** — connecting AI into the tools and workflows a business already uses
- **Custom systems** — building tailored solutions when existing products don't fit
- **Practical adoption** — helping teams move from "AI is interesting" to "AI is working for us"

### This organization

Some of our repositories are public — tools, experiments, and things we think
are worth sharing. Others are private client work. What you see here is the
public side of what we build.
